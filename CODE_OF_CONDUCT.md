# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TYPE/262 to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone who takes part.
That holds regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and speak in ways that keep this an open, friendly and healthy community.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and take feedback the same way.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels. Everyone was new once.
- Respect privacy. Many people here work under a pseudonym; never try to identify them.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Own your mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission, including linking a wallet or a pseudonym to a real identity.
- Scams, phishing links, fake airdrops, impersonating maintainers, and unsolicited token promotion.
- Spam, trolling, and repeated off-topic disruption.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, code review, discussions, and anywhere someone represents TYPE/262 in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through [private reporting](https://github.com/Type262/262/security/advisories/new).
Title the report **Conduct report** and pick any severity; we close it without publishing. Include what happened, where (links help), when, and who was involved.

Every report is reviewed promptly and handled in confidence.
A maintainer who is part of a report takes no part in handling it.
Nobody faces retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code.
They choose a response that fits the situation:

| Response | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Correction | Unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour | A private note explaining the problem. A public apology may be requested. |
| Warning | A single serious incident, or repeated minor ones | A warning with clear consequences for continuing. No contact with the people involved for a set time. |
| Temporary ban | A serious breach, or continued behaviour after a warning | No interaction with the project for a set time. |
| Permanent ban | A pattern of breaches, harassment, or aggression toward any group | No interaction with the project at all. |
