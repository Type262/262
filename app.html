          02 — Pay
          
Pay a name.

          
Send to a domain instead of an address. We resolve its WALLET record and check DNSSEC, then your wallet sends USDC or SOL on Solana, or USDG or ETH on Robinhood Chain.

          03 — Escrow
          
Pay them first.

          
Pay a domain that hasn't published a wallet yet. The money waits on-chain until the owner claims it with one DNS record. Unclaimed after 180 days? You get it back.

          06 — Badge
          
Get paid anywhere.

          
A "pay via DNS" button for your site. It opens a pay page that resolves your domain live, so nobody can swap the address in your HTML.

        

        

        

      

      
      

        

          07 — Launch
          
Launch for a domain.

          
Launch a token whose creator fees go to a domain instead of a wallet. The domain's owner claims them with one DNS record. On Solana it's pump.fun; on Robinhood Chain, pons.
